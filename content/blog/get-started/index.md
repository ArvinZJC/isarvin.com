Here's a belated Spring Festival greeting from the new blogger. Yes, it's me. <mark>Wishing us all a prosperous and joyful Year of the Snake!</mark> 🐍

I published my personal site in late 2021. Around the same time, I planned to start blogging. Unfortunately, I only got round to it today, more than three years later. So why the delay, and why start now?

- Procrastination. Definitely! 🙄
- Tired of social media sharing. Maybe a reflection of being an introvert? 👀
- A growing desire to **record** guides, ideas, and thoughts. Never too late to learn; never too late to blog. 🤠

You might notice I put "record" rather than "share". I'd be glad if my blog helped you, but I'm not here to tell anyone the right or best way to do things. Mostly, I'm exploring a different way to take notes. My first priority, at least for now, is to leave something for my future self to look back on.

So when will I post again? 💆‍♂️
